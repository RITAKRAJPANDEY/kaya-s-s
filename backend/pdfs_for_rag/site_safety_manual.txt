APEX BUILDERS GROUP - GENERAL SITE SAFETY MANUAL (REVISION 2026.1)
DOCUMENT ID: ABG-SSM-2026
EFFECTIVE DATE: JANUARY 15, 2026

SECTION 1: GENERAL SITE RULES & ACCESS
1.1 All personnel, contractors, and visitors entering the construction perimeter must complete the mandatory Site Safety Induction.
1.2 Working hours are 07:00 to 18:00. Any night work or weekend shifts require a written Permit to Work (PTW).
1.3 Smoking, vaping, alcohol, and non-prescribed controlled substances are strictly prohibited across the entire project perimeter.
1.4 Cell phone usage is prohibited while operating machinery, walking through designated transit corridors, or working at elevation.

SECTION 2: WORKING AT HEIGHT & FALL PROTECTION
2.1 Fall protection is mandatory for any work where a worker can fall 6 feet (1.8 meters) or more to a lower level.
2.2 Acceptable fall protection systems include:
    a) Guardrail systems with top rails at 42 inches (+/- 3 inches), midrails at 21 inches, and toeboards at least 3.5 inches high.
    b) Personal Fall Arrest Systems (PFAS), including a full-body harness, shock-absorbing lanyard or self-retracting lifeline (SRL), and an approved anchor point rated for at least 5,000 lbs (22.2 kN) per attached worker.
    c) Safety net systems installed within 30 feet below the working level.
2.3 Floor openings, roof hatches, and floor penetrations greater than 2 inches in diameter must be securely covered with load-bearing covers marked "HOLE" or "COVER" or protected by standard guardrails.
2.4 When working within 6 feet of an unprotected edge, a 100% tie-off policy is strictly enforced.

SECTION 3: ELECTRICAL SAFETY & LOCKOUT/TAGOUT (LOTO)
3.1 All temporary power distribution boards must feature Ground Fault Circuit Interrupters (GFCI) or 30mA RCD protection.
3.2 Only certified electricians are permitted to access breaker panels, switchboards, or perform live electrical troubleshooting.
3.3 Minimum approach distance to overhead power lines up to 50kV is 10 feet (3.0 meters). For voltages exceeding 50kV, add 4 inches for every additional 10kV.
3.4 Lockout/Tagout (LOTO) procedures must be applied before servicing any energized equipment, mechanical systems, or high-pressure lines. Each worker must affix their personal padlock and danger tag.

SECTION 4: EXCAVATION AND TRENCHING SAFETY
4.1 Any trench or excavation measuring 5 feet (1.5 meters) or deeper must be protected by an approved protective system: sloping, benching, shoring, or trench shields/boxes.
4.2 Daily pre-entry inspections must be conducted by a Designated Competent Person prior to worker entry and after any rain event or seismic vibration.
4.3 Spoil piles and heavy equipment must be placed at least 2 feet (0.6 meters) away from the edge of the excavation.
4.4 Ladders or stairways must be provided in trenches 4 feet or deeper, located within 25 feet (7.6 meters) of lateral travel distance for all workers.

SECTION 5: EMERGENCY RESPONSE AND INCIDENT REPORTING
5.1 In the event of a medical emergency, call the Site Emergency Dispatch at 555-0199 immediately.
5.2 First aid stations and automated external defibrillators (AEDs) are located in the Site Management Trailer and Ground Floor Tool Crib.
5.3 All near-misses, minor injuries, and property damage incidents must be reported to the Site Safety Officer within 2 hours of occurrence.
