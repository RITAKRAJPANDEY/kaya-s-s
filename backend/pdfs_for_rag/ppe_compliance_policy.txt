MANDATORY PERSONAL PROTECTIVE EQUIPMENT (PPE) POLICY
POLICY REF: POL-PPE-2026-REV4
APEX BUILDERS GROUP SAFETY & HEALTH MANUAL

1. POLICY OVERVIEW
All workers, supervisors, trade subcontractors, engineers, and authorized visitors must wear mandatory Personal Protective Equipment (PPE) at all times within the active construction boundary. Zero tolerance is enforced for non-compliance.

2. MINIMUM MANDATORY PPE (THE FIVE ESSENTIALS)
2.1 Head Protection (Hard Hat):
    - Must comply with ANSI/ISEA Z89.1 (Type 1, Class E or Class G).
    - Hard hats must be worn forward-facing with chin straps fastened when working at height, in windy conditions, or near crane pick zones.
    - Helmets must be free from cracks, structural deformation, and chemical solvent damage. Stickers must not obscure inspection areas.
2.2 Eye and Face Protection:
    - High-impact safety glasses conforming to ANSI Z87.1+ with integrated side shields are required 100% of the time.
    - Full-face shields and safety goggles must be worn during cutting, grinding, welding chipping, chemical mixing, or high-pressure washing.
2.3 High-Visibility Apparel:
    - High-visibility safety vests, shirts, or jackets conforming to ANSI/ISEA 107 Class 2 (daytime) or Class 3 (night shifts and traffic control).
    - Color must be fluorescent yellow-green or fluorescent orange-red with retroreflective silver striping.
2.4 Foot Protection:
    - Safety boots complying with ASTM F2413 or EN ISO 20345 standard.
    - Must feature steel or composite protective toe caps, puncture-resistant soles, and minimum 6-inch ankle support. Soft shoes, sneakers, and sandals are strictly forbidden.
2.5 Hand Protection:
    - Task-specific safety gloves must be worn whenever handling sharp materials, chemicals, rebar, sheet metal, or power tools. Cut-level A4 or higher required for steel framing and glass handling.

3. SPECIALIZED PPE REQUIREMENTS
3.1 Hearing Protection:
    - Earplugs or earmuffs rated with Noise Reduction Rating (NRR) of at least 25 dB must be worn in zones where noise levels exceed 85 dBA (e.g., jackhammers, concrete saws, generators).
3.2 Respiratory Protection:
    - NIOSH-approved N95 or P100 half-mask respirators are required during dry concrete cutting, drywall sanding, silica-generating operations, or toxic fume exposure.
3.3 Welding PPE:
    - Approved auto-darkening welding helmet with shade filter 9 to 13, fire-resistant leather sleeves, and split-cowhide welding gloves.

4. PPE ENFORCEMENT & DISCIPLINARY POLICY
- First Offense: Formal verbal safety warning and immediate compliance requirement.
- Second Offense: Written safety violation notice and 1-day site suspension.
- Third Offense: Permanent revocation of site access and removal from project.
